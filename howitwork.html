HOW IT WORKS

Reporting a cleanliness problem takes only a few simple steps.

📸

1. SPOT AN ISSUE

Notice garbage, an unclean washroom, a water problem, or any other cleanliness issue on campus.

📝

2. REPORT IT

Open the report form and provide the issue category, location, description, and an optional photo.

📨

3. SUBMIT

Submit your report. Your report is saved and can be viewed in your report history.

🔧

4. ISSUE RESOLVED

The responsible team can review the issue and take action to resolve the problem.
